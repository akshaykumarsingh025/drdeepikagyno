Privacy Policy

Nirog Naari mobile app · Effective date: 12 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Nirog Naari app ("the app", "we", "us") collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it. The app is published by AKSHAY KUMAR SINGH in association with the clinic of Dr. Deepika Singh, F-11, South Extension Part 1, New Delhi – 110049, India.

The app gives general health information and helps you keep track of your health. It does not replace a doctor. We never sell your data, and we never use your health information for advertising.

1. Information we collect

WhatWhy
Account details – name, email address, and your Google account name and photo if you sign in with GoogleTo create and secure your account
Patient details – name, age, phone number and similar details you enter in your profile (you can add family members as separate profiles)To book consultations and so the doctor knows who she is treating
Health information you enter – period and pregnancy dates, symptoms, readings such as weight, blood pressure and sugar, medicines, and the values from lab reports you scan or uploadTo provide tracking, reminders, report explanations, trends and diet plans
Appointments and prescriptions – booking time, reason for visit, and prescriptions written by the doctorTo provide online and clinic consultations

Your contact choice – whether you agreed to receive health tips and offersSo we only send offers to people who asked for them
Device information – device identifiers, notification token, advertising ID, crash reportsTo send reminders and alerts, show ads, and fix crashes

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, call logs or SMS.

2. Online AI features (only with your permission)

AI report explanations, the report chat, diet plans and the symptom checker can send the health details you enter to online AI services (OpenRouter, Groq and Google Gemini) to create an answer. The app asks for your permission before it does this, and you can turn it off at any time in More > AI Data Sharing. If you say no, these AI features will not work, but all other features keep working.

AI answers can be wrong. Always check important health decisions with a doctor.

3. Who we share information with

We share information only as needed to run the app:

  • The treating doctor (Dr. Deepika Singh and her clinic staff) – your patient details, appointment, the reason for your visit and the reports you choose to share, so she can consult you.
  • Family members you invite – if you turn on Family Alerts, the person you share a code with is told when a dose is missed or a reading is unusual. You can remove them at any time.
  • Service providers who process data for us: Google Firebase (sign-in, database, notifications, crash reports), Cloudflare (our server), Razorpay (payments), LiveKit (video calls), OpenRouter, Groq and Google Gemini (AI answers, only with your permission), and Google Sheets (clinic visit bookings, so the clinic can call you back).
  • Google AdMob – shows an ad only when you choose to watch one for extra AI answers, and may use your advertising ID and device information. Google tells our server when an ad has been watched, together with your account ID, so we can add the credit. Your health information is never shared with AdMob. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads.
  • When required by law – if a court or government authority lawfully requires it.

We do not sell your personal or health information.

4. Messages from us

We may contact you about your appointments, prescriptions, payments, or a concern you raised, by phone, WhatsApp, SMS or email. This is part of the service.

We send health tips and offers only if you agree, either when you set up your profile or in More > Health tips and offers. You can turn this off at any time in the same place, and we will stop.

5. How we protect your information

  • All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Your records are linked to your account, and security rules stop other users from reading them.
  • Payments are checked on our server, and payment details stay with Razorpay.
  • You can lock the app with your fingerprint or a PIN.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, health data, reports and reminders are deleted. We may keep payment and consultation records for as long as Indian law requires (for example, for tax or medical record-keeping). Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.

7. Delete your account and data

In the app: open Profile > Delete Account > Delete Forever. This permanently deletes your account, reports and health data.

Without the app: email akshaykumar.singh025@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Nirog Naari account". We will delete your account and data within 30 days and confirm by email.

8. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw your consent, and name someone to act for you. Most of this you can do in the app; for anything else, write to us at the email below.

9. Children

The app is meant for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. A parent or guardian may add a child's details as a family profile and is responsible for that information.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
