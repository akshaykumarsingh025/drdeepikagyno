============================================================
WHATSAPP MESSAGES SENT FROM WEBSITE
============================================================
Placeholders filled at runtime:
  {username}  = user's entered name
  {phone}     = user's phone number
  {height}    = user's height in cm
  {weight}    = user's weight in kg
  {bmi}       = calculated BMI (e.g. 22.5)
============================================================

1. FROM OTHER TOOLS (PCOS Score, Fertility Score, Due Date)
   Function: requestDietPlan(planName)
   Dr. Deepika sends the matching PDF from public/diet-plans/
