Finding the right gynecologist is crucial for a woman's health journey. If you reside in South Delhi, you have access to one of the most distinguished medical professionals in the region. Why Dr. Deepika Singh is South Delhi's Trusted Gynecologist

Dr. Deepika Singh's reputation is built on a foundation of exceptional medical education and over 15 years of diverse clinical experience. Her MD from AIIMS New Delhi ensures a rigorous, evidence-based approach to medicine.

She understands that gynecological visits can be stressful. Therefore, she prioritizes creating a safe, non-judgmental environment where patients feel heard. Her holistic approach means she looks beyond immediate symptoms to address underlying causes, offering tailored treatment plans.

Services at Our South Extension Clinic

Our clinic is equipped to handle a wide range of women's health needs:

  • Pregnancy Care: Comprehensive antenatal care, expert management of high-risk pregnancies, and compassionate support through delivery.
  • Gynaecological Surgeries: As an FCLS-certified surgeon, Dr. Deepika specializes in minimally invasive laparoscopic and hysteroscopic surgeries for fibroids, cysts, and endometriosis.
  • Infertility Treatment: Personalized evaluation and treatments, including ovulation induction and IUI, to help you build your family.
  • PCOS/PCOD Management: Holistic treatment addressing hormonal imbalances, weight management, and related fertility issues.
  • Menopause Clinic: Dedicated care for navigating the physical and emotional changes of menopause, including HRT.
  • Cancer Screening: Routine Pap smears, HPV testing, and clinical breast examinations for early detection.
  • Cosmetic Gynecology: Advanced, confidential procedures to restore function and confidence.
