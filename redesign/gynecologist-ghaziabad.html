About Your Doctor

Top Gynecologist for Ghaziabad – Dr. Deepika Singh

Dedicated to empowering women through compassionate healthcare and expert medical guidance.

When dealing with sensitive women's health issues or navigating a pregnancy, you need a doctor you can trust implicitly. For many women in Ghaziabad, that trust is placed in Dr. Deepika Singh, a highly experienced, AIIMS-trained gynecologist practicing in South Delhi.

Why Ghaziabad Patients Trust Dr. Deepika Singh

Dr. Deepika Singh brings over 15 years of clinical excellence to her practice. Her extensive training, including an MD from AIIMS New Delhi and FCLS certification in laparoscopic surgery, enables her to handle everything from routine check-ups to complex surgical interventions and high-risk pregnancies. Her patient-first philosophy ensures that every woman receives personalized, empathetic care.

How to Reach Us from Ghaziabad

Our clinic, located at F-11, South Extension Part 1, is easily accessible from Ghaziabad thanks to improved highway infrastructure:

  • Via Delhi-Meerut Expressway (NH-24): This expressway offers a smooth, fast route straight into central Delhi, connecting to the Ring Road. Depending on traffic, the drive can take 40-50 minutes.
  • Via Metro: The Red or Blue lines from Ghaziabad offer convenient connections to the Yellow/Pink lines, dropping you close to the clinic at INA or South Extension stations.
