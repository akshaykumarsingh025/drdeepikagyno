All 19 Health Tools

Every tool is free, needs no sign-up, and produces a PDF report you can take to any doctor.

Periods & Cycle

  • Heavy Period Checker — measure your menstrual blood loss with the pictorial chart used in clinics to diagnose heavy menstrual bleeding.
  • Period Pain & Endometriosis Checker — screen severe period pain against the recognised warning signs for endometriosis and adenomyosis.
  • PCOS Risk Screener — 10-question polycystic ovary syndrome risk assessment based on Rotterdam criteria.
  • Period & Ovulation Tracker — predict your next period, ovulation day and fertile window.
  • PMS & PMDD Screener — check severe premenstrual mood symptoms against DSM-5 criteria for PMDD.
  • Teen Period Checker — for ages 10 to 19: what is normal for your age, and the few things worth seeing a doctor about.

Pregnancy & After

  • Due Date Calculator — estimated delivery date, current pregnancy week, trimester and milestones.
  • Antenatal Care Planner — a complete pregnancy calendar of visits, scans, blood tests and vaccinations with real dates.
  • Pregnancy Weight Gain Tracker — check your weight gain against evidence-based targets for your pre-pregnancy BMI.
  • Gestational Diabetes Risk — risk of diabetes in pregnancy and exactly when you should have the glucose test.
  • Postnatal Mood Check — a confidential 10-question check for postnatal depression in new mothers.

Screening & Planning

  • Cervical Screening Checker — when your next Pap smear or HPV test is due, and whether the HPV vaccine still applies to you.
  • Contraception Method Finder — match every birth control method against your health history using WHO medical eligibility criteria.
  • Fertility Score — a fertility wellness score from age, cycles, lifestyle and hormone results such as AMH and FSH.
  • Menopause Symptom Checker — score hot flushes, sleep, mood and urogenital symptoms across 11 areas.

Body & Wellness

  • Anaemia Risk Screener — iron-deficiency risk from symptoms, periods and diet, with haemoglobin interpretation.
  • Thyroid Symptom Checker — underactive versus overactive thyroid symptoms, plus TSH interpretation.
  • BMI & PCOS Weight Risk — BMI using the Asia-Pacific cut-offs that apply to Indian women, with metabolic risk scoring.
  • Personalised Diet Plans — PCOS, pregnancy, postnatal and weight loss plans matched to your BMI and food preference.

A tool can screen. Only a doctor can diagnose.

Every tool here is designed to tell you whether something is worth checking — not to replace an examination. If a result concerned you, bring the report along.
